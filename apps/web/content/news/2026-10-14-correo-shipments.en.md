In the **Correo Argentino** section, when you add a tracking you choose the **shipment type**, as on the post office site, and the form shows exactly the fields that type needs.

## Supported types

- (Plus) Argentina → Argentina and ordinary shipments within Argentina: a prefix and a number of 7–9 digits;
- Argentina → another country: a prefix and a number;
- another country → Argentina: a code of 13 characters, for example `AB123456789CD`;
- DNI and Pasaporte: a procedure number;
- Mercado Libre and e-commerce parcels: a shipment number.

Before adding, we check the number against the rules of the chosen type, and notifications arrive like for the other trackings: when a shipment gets a new movement.
